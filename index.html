    HOW TO READ THE EXPERIMENT

    Two views of the same position.

    The GPT gets a move list. It was trained from random weights on synthetic Reversi games. Each square is a token, like a word in autocomplete.

    The engine gets the board. It tracks the discs and knows the legal moves. Filtering removes illegal choices and rescales the remaining probabilities. It leaves the model’s weights alone.
